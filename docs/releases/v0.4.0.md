# Omarchy Arcade v0.4.0 — It’s all downhill from here

Release candidate notes. v0.4.0 has not been published yet.

FreeSki joins Arcade. Fourteen games in one native window, and now there’s a yeti to worry about.

- **Find your feet.** A 1,200-metre Practice slope introduces carving, braking, jumps and crash recovery.
- **Find your limit.** Free Ski generates an endless mountain. Turn on creature pursuit for a chase after 1,000 metres, or leave it off and ski at your own pace. The two modes keep separate distance records.
- **Find a better line.** Five Slalom Cup courses unlock in order. Miss a gate and add five seconds; finish to earn a medal and open the next course.
- **Come back later.** Pause, close the app or return to Arcade. Your run saves locally and reopens paused, including jumps and pursuit state.

Steer with **A/D**, **Left/Right** or the mouse. **F** toggles the fast tuck. Brake with **S/Down**, the right mouse button on the slope or the on-screen brake button. **Esc** pauses; **Ctrl+H** returns to Arcade. Space keeps its normal role activating focused buttons.

Thanks to [MayberryDT](https://github.com/MayberryDT) for [FreeSki](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-retro-arcade/pull/42), including its original artwork, sound and detailed playtest evidence. All thirteen existing games remain, with offline play and bundled Stockfish for Chess.

## Get playing

Once v0.4.0 is published, download its x86_64 package and `SHA256SUMS` into the same directory. Close Arcade and back up your saves before upgrading.

```sh
sha256sum --ignore-missing --check SHA256SUMS
sudo pacman -U ./omarchy-retro-arcade-0.4.0-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst
omarchy-retro-arcade --game freeski
```

The versioned build should report `Omarchy Arcade 0.4.0` from `omarchy-retro-arcade --version`.

## Saves and rollback

Existing games retain their save locations and schemas. FreeSki uses `$XDG_STATE_HOME/omarchy-retro-arcade/freeski.json`, normally `~/.local/state/omarchy-retro-arcade/freeski.json`. Unsupported or corrupt saves remain untouched until explicit recovery. Supported earlier FreeSki development saves retain their original files during migration; earlier-rule records remain separate.

Pacman handles updates. To downgrade, close Arcade and run `sudo pacman -U /path/to/previous.pkg.tar.zst`. v0.3.0 has no FreeSki and ignores its save; keep that file for a later upgrade. A package downgrade does not reverse save migrations. Keep matching backups. Uninstall with `sudo pacman -R omarchy-retro-arcade`; retain state directories to keep progress.

## Build evidence and limits

FreeSki’s reviewed input, `899e292`, passed the [native, Arch and Tanks CI jobs](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-retro-arcade/actions/runs/35192203712). These covered workspace tests with real Stockfish, fourteen-game switching, FreeSki suspended runs and dark/light/compact/200% layouts, package installation and save preservation across a same-package reinstall. That evidence predates this version bump; final v0.4.0 CI and asset verification are tracked in the [release checklist](v0.4.0-checklist.md).

The contributor recorded [hands-on FreeSki acceptance](../../games/freeski/docs/PLAYTESTS.md) and earlier package/Wayland checks. These are historical development-build results, not a fresh acceptance run of the final v0.4.0 package. The complete v0.3.0-to-v0.4.0 upgrade matrix, collection-wide Omarchy/Wayland audio and gameplay acceptance, and aarch64 remain unverified. The binary release target is x86_64 on Omarchy 4 / Hyprland; this is a community app.

The release bundle will contain the player package, corresponding Arcade and pinned Stockfish source with its evaluation network, `RELEASE-NOTES.md`, `BUILD.txt` and `SHA256SUMS`. `BUILD.txt` must identify the exact source commit, tree and build run used for the published package.
