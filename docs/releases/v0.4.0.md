# Omarchy Arcade v0.4.0: It’s all downhill from here

![FreeSki: fast mode with a creature in pursuit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcballard/omarchy-retro-arcade/7d6e5f42b2e451ea08649eca577675990e58e9d8/games/freeski/assets/shelf.png)

FreeSki is here. Arcade now has fourteen games in one native window.

- **Find your feet.** A 1,200-metre Practice slope with carving, braking, ramps and crash recovery.
- **Keep going.** An endless mountain, optional yeti pursuit and fast mode. Press F. See how that goes.
- **Race the gates.** Five Slalom courses, local medals and five-second penalties for missed gates.
- **Come back later.** Runs save locally and reopen paused. Your records, preferences and course unlocks stay with you.

Steer with A/D, the arrow keys or the mouse. Brake with S, Down or the right mouse button. Ramps launch automatically; Esc pauses and Ctrl+H returns to Arcade.

Thanks to [MayberryDT](https://github.com/MayberryDT) for [bringing FreeSki to Arcade](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-retro-arcade/pull/42).

All thirteen existing games remain, including bundled Stockfish for computer Chess. This release also tidies up focus handling, stops Chess/Scram/Invaders audio workers when leaving a game, and keeps normal Pinball shutdown responsive.

## Get skiing

Download the x86_64 package and `SHA256SUMS` below. Close Arcade and back up your saves before upgrading.

```sh
sha256sum --ignore-missing --check SHA256SUMS &&
sudo pacman -U ./omarchy-retro-arcade-0.4.0-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst
omarchy-retro-arcade --game freeski
```

You can also open Arcade from your app launcher. `omarchy-retro-arcade --version` reports `Omarchy Arcade 0.4.0`.
